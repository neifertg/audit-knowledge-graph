Audit risk hides between the tables. This is what it looks like when you connect them.

A synthetic US regional bank keeps its risks in the ERM register and in Internal Audit's risk and control matrices. They describe the same business in different words. Reconciled into a single framework and connected as a graph, they show exposure no table holds on its own.

Synthetic data throughout; descriptions AI-written ahead of time and committed; no real institution, person or vendor.
