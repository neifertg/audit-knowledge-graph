Audit planning results from a reconciled risk and control map

A synthetic US regional bank keeps its risks in the ERM register and in Internal Audit's risk and control matrices. Both lists were reconciled into a single framework, then loaded into a relational database and a graph. The notes below are planning consequences of the published results.

Synthetic data throughout; descriptions AI-written ahead of time and committed; no real institution, person or vendor.

Fourth-party vendor exposure

With a register of which vendors rely on which others, {{derived:featured.vendor_rows}} auditable entities are exposed to {{derived:featured.vendor}}. It is the vendor the most others rely on, directly or further out, with ties broken by name. {{derived:fourth_party.direct}} of them perform a process that runs on a system that vendor supplies. {{derived:fourth_party.fourth_party_only}} have no such link and appear only through another vendor's reliance. Those are the entities a direct-vendor list would miss.

Does a graph beat SQL?

Asked {{derived:rounds_count|count_word}} planning questions in both, with the same facts and answers proved equal: SQL wins {{derived:verdict_counts.SQL|times_word}}, the graph {{derived:verdict_counts.graph|times_word}}, and {{derived:verdict_counts.close|count_word}} are close. The difference is how much query each needs, not speed: no speed claims are made at demo scale. The scoring rule, the symmetry table and both queries.

What was merged, and what is waiting

Auto-merge uses a training-bank precision target of {{scores.json:threshold_targets.auto_accept_training_precision}}. Merged precision is {{scores.json:risk.precision.count}} for risks and {{scores.json:control.precision.count}} for controls. Merged recall is {{scores.json:risk.recall.count}} for risks and {{scores.json:control.recall.count}} for controls. If a reviewer resolves the queue correctly, recall would be {{scores.json:risk.recall_if_resolved.count}} for risks and {{scores.json:control.recall_if_resolved.count}} for controls. The review queue holds {{scores.json:review_queue.total}} pairs. The matcher's thresholds are deliberately conservative, so most true matches wait in the review queue rather than being merged silently.

The data is synthetic, from a single generator and a single bank, with small samples. The training bank comes from the same generator, so its weights know the generator's habits. No speed or scale claim is made.

The graph informs auditor judgment; it does not score risk or set the audit plan.
