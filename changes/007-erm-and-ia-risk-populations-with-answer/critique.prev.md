VERDICT: REVISE
1. [major] Nothing stops two different risks in one source from being identical rows, and the theme-by-context model makes that likely. The plan says "A concept instance is a theme applied to one auditable entity" and "ERM links at business-unit breadth (every entity in the owning business unit)", and a theme's instances sit "mostly on sibling entities of one business unit". If the context slot does not change the text of both sources (for example, ERM writes at business-unit level and leaves the entity out), two ERM instances of one theme in the same unit get the same title, description, L3 node and exposure set, yet sit in different clusters (or none). E2 would rightly flag them as within-ERM duplicates and be scored wrong, which corrupts the duplicate precision that O3 publishes. The same holds for an IA-only instance and a matched one. No criterion catches it: AC-4's TF-IDF band, hard-negative rate and a's leakage rules only look at cross-source pairs and matched pairs. -> State in Data impact that the context appears in both sources' title or description. Add to AC-3 or AC-4 (and its test) that no two risks of one source in different clusters, or where either has `none`, share an identical title plus description. A cosine ceiling for such pairs would do the same job.
2. [minor] The placement rule does not fit the full-size organization. There are 31 business units for 81 entities: 4 units have 1 entity, 13 have 2 and 8 have 3. AC-1's counts imply roughly 225 distinct instances over about 45 themes, about 5 per theme. Because instances are unique by (theme, entity), most themes cannot sit "mostly on sibling entities of one business unit". They will spread over 2-3 units, so fewer near-misses survive AC-5 (b)'s business-unit filter. AC-5 (b) (F1 at most 0.80) and (c) (at least 30% same-unit hard negatives) depend on this. -> Reword it as "spread over the fewest units that hold them, with at least 2 instances in one unit". Include the same-unit near-miss share in slice 1's measurement, so the 2-hour checkpoint sees this conflict before the remaining themes are written.
3. [minor] AC-5 (b) and (c) can be read two ways. "takes the best one above the best global threshold" does not say whether the threshold is the AC-4 TF-IDF baseline's best threshold or one tuned for the combined rule. Tuning it for the combined rule is the stricter reading. "at least 30% of IA risks' hard negatives share the IA risk's business unit" does not give the denominator: all hard-negative pairs, or the IA risks that have a hard negative and at least one of them in their own unit. A test author could pick whichever reading passes. -> Name the threshold (the combined rule's own best) and the denominator in the criterion.
4. [minor] Audit and issue linking at full size has no test. The plan says "Audits and issues at full size ... with the epic's linking rules intact (IA issues link only to controls their audit tested)". Today's audit code builds scope from `c.entities` per concept, and issues look up `by_key` over `lib.RISK_CONCEPTS`. Both must be rewired to per-instance keys and single-entity IA exposure. No test, at sample or full size, checks either the audit-scope rule (a tested control mitigates a risk that a covered entity is exposed to) or the issue rule. Child e owns those tests, but this change is the one that rewires the code. -> Add both assertions to AC-3's helper at full size, or say plainly that they stay untested until e.
5. [minor] The timebox exceeds the epic's allocation. The epic gives "b-e: about 2 each" within an 11-day total; this plan takes 2.5. -> Accept it and note the half-day overrun against the epic total, or trim. Keep the 2-hour checkpoint either way.
Not checked: whether templated per-instance text can hit AC-4's TF-IDF band (0.40-0.75) and AC-5 (b)'s cap at the same time (only a build will show it; the Risks section's re-plan rule covers it); whether 50 ERM L3 nodes, and IA's L3 nodes, can absorb about 45 themes with 10-20% misfiling without new nodes; whether rating agreement between matched pairs (75% today) is a meaningful extra cue for E2.
