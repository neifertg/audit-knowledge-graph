1. [major] The canonical rule does not cover controls, and the unit the headline figures are scored on is not stated. "Connected components of merged edges become canonical items" has one guard: "A component may hold at most one IA risk per ERM duplicate group. If one IA item clears auto-accept against two ERM items ..." That guard is about risks and ERM duplicate groups. Controls have no duplicate groups, and their truth is "one ERM control plus every IA control" ("no cluster holds two ERM controls"). Many IA controls legitimately join one ERM control, so components chain: IA control A merges with ERM X, IA control B merges with ERM X and ERM Y, and X and Y become one canonical control. This is likely, not just possible. ERM generic controls are one per theme and kind, and the citation cue is ERM's *subject phrase*, which every kind of a theme shares. So an IA generic control can clear auto-accept against several ERM siblings. The first sub-rule is also ambiguous: "at most one IA risk per ERM duplicate group" limits IA risks per group, but the sentence after it is about one IA risk reaching two ERM risks. Last, child c reports "cross-source risk and control matching: precision, recall". It does not say whether that is per scored edge or per pair implied by the final canonical items. Transitive merging makes the two differ, and the page should publish the second. -> Add the control rule: a canonical control holds at most one ERM control. An IA control whose auto-accepted edges reach two ERM controls keeps only its highest-scoring edge and sends the others to the queue. State a deterministic tie-break, for example by score and then by sorted IDs. Reword the risk rule so it says what is limited. State that c scores the pairs implied by the final canonical assignment. Extend the whole-output INV-3 test to assert "no canonical control holds two ERM controls" and "no canonical risk holds two ERM risks that are not an auto-accepted duplicate pair".

[minor] The training bank has to use templated text, but the epic does not say so. `akg build --seed 7 --size full` fails ("AI text is stale for key risk_erm:...") because AI text exists only for seed 42.
