VERDICT: REVISE
1. [blocker] The 55-65% IA control overlap is still not reachable. AC-1 requires "55-65% of IA controls share a true cluster with an ERM control" (the epic requires it too). The data model says "ERM records generic controls per theme per business unit" and "Kinds are drawn independently by each source". AC-5 adds that the number of controls on an ERM risk must not depend on whether IA holds the instance. I checked this against the seed-42 risk population (007's selection code, re-run outside the repo). It has 90 shared, 77 ERM-only, 60 IA-only and 13 duplicate instances, 31 business units, and 141 ERM (theme, business unit) groups. Only 87 of those groups also hold an IA instance, covering 108 of the 150 IA risks.
   - **Budget.** ERM has 167 specific controls, so about 300 - 167 - ~14 orphans = ~119 generic controls, or 0.84 per group. IA has 150 specific controls and ~232 generic ones, about 1.55 per instance.
   - **What 55% needs.** At IA = 400, 55% is 220 matched IA controls. The 90 shared specific controls cover part of that, so about 130 IA generic controls must match.
   - **Upper bound.** One IA generic matches only an ERM generic of the same kind in its group. ERM generics have to be spread evenly over groups, or the control count would say whether IA holds the risk, which AC-5 forbids. Even if every IA kind agreed with ERM's, at most ~91 IA generics could match. Orphans sitting in clusters add at most ~10-19.
   - **Result.** The best case is about 200 of 400 matched (50%). With kinds drawn independently from one skewed mix of 8 kinds, agreement is about 0.3-0.5, which gives roughly 33-40%. Even the most favourable corner of the bands (ERM 330, IA 360, perfect agreement) only just reaches 55%, and independent draws cannot.
   - **Fix.** Change the structure, not the band. Make ERM generic groups coarser, so each ERM generic covers many IA instances: for example per theme bank-wide, or per kind per business unit across themes. With ~119 ERM generics over 56 themes, each theme gets about 2.1 kinds, which allows up to ~300 IA matches. Also let IA choose its generic kinds from ERM's kinds for that group when one exists, keeping IA's count independent. Put this arithmetic in the plan, and confirm it at the 2-hour checkpoint before writing anything else.
2. [major] "IA records them per auditable entity" contradicts "An IA generic control matches the ERM generic control of the same kind for its theme and business unit". The 66 IA entities hold about 2.3 themes each. If IA generic controls really are per entity, one control spans several themes. It would then match several ERM generics, which breaks "no cluster holds two ERM controls". -> State the unit exactly: per (entity, theme), which is per IA risk instance, or per entity with one named theme. Also say which theme subject the wording uses.
3. [major] The last sentence of AC-5 ("The number of controls on an ERM risk does not depend on whether IA holds the same risk instance") has no measure, so the test cannot fail on it. The reverse cue is not covered at all: IA's control count, or which kinds it has, predicting whether ERM holds the instance. Fix 1 would make that reverse cue more likely. -> Add a concrete check in both directions, computed from published files plus the answer key, and give it a threshold. For example: per source, the best "count >= k" or "has kind k" predictor of "the other source holds this instance" scores F1 below 0.5. Alternatively, mean control counts differ by less than 0.2 between held and not-held instances.
4. [major] The AC-2 scenario path is still ambiguous on two points.
   - "(own source's links)" does not say whether a system's entity reach is the union over ERM and IA controls or is computed per source. ERM exposure is unit-wide plus 10-20% stale links, so an ERM path inflates reach, and the ranking changes with the choice.
   - With 30 systems and integer reach, ties at rank 3 are likely, and the plan gives no tie-break.
   E3's blast-radius round and the recomputation test both need one answer. -> Define the reach as the union over both sources (or name the per-source alternative) and give a deterministic tie-break, such as reach, then business units, then system_id.
