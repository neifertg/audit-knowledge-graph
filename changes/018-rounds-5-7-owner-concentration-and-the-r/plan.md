## Why
E3 child d (O2). It completes the seven head-to-head rounds, adds the page's second story (repeat findings in disguise) and third (owner concentration, derived), and produces `rounds.json`, the single file E4's page reads. Every number in it comes from running both engines, with the checks that prove it. It also makes the verdict rule symmetric, so the comparison a data scientist reads is not tilted.

## Data impact
- **No published, reconciled or store schema change.** New committed files: `fourth_party.csv` (8 rows at prototype; fictional vendor names from the existing list, INV-9) and `rounds.json`.
- **Round 5's overlay is transient** in both stores (TEMP table on a read-only connection; a rolled-back Neo4j transaction) and leaves the data, and so INV-8 parity, untouched (AC-3).
- **Pipeline:** step 5 of `akg build` (export) now exists, completing the epic's contract.
- **INV-6:** neither the rounds, the oracle nor the export reads the answer key (tested on a copy without it); scenario outcomes are claims verified by named tests.
- docs/data-model.md gains the rounds 5-7 and export entries and the updated verdict rule and figures.

## Risks
- **The verdict rule changes after 017 published under the old one** (owner decision, 2026-11-21). The old rule counted a SQL join twice, a fixed Cypher path once, and a recursive CTE three times against Cypher's one. The two critiques of this plan found these one at a time; the symmetric rule removes all three, and every figure was re-measured before approval (L-018). Rounds 3 and 4 change from `graph` to `close`, and round 5 measures `SQL` (expected graph). The docs say so.
- **What the rule cannot measure** (SQL's ubiquity, tooling, team familiarity) stays out of it and goes to E4's pros and cons; every component is published so a reader can re-score.
- **`OPTIONAL MATCH` stays a clause** while `LEFT JOIN` is not: pinned and disclosed (owner concentration would be 10 vs 8 `graph` without it, rather than `close`).
- **Three verdicts are within one point of the line:** round 7 exactly on it (10 vs 8), rounds 1 (9 vs 7) and 3 (8 vs 7). A one-clause change flips each. Every margin is published and these three are named; nothing is tuned.
- **Round 5's story depends on a generated register.** The vendor rule and seed are pinned in this plan, and the discrimination check (AC-2) fails an empty or all-direct answer.
- **Neo4j transaction semantics:** no schema in the overlay; rollback in `finally`, tested on failure on both engines.
- **Account lockout:** the wrong-password test makes exactly one attempt.
- **Slow suite on this machine (L-017):** the new queries are sub-second.
