AC-5's "round 5 29 vs 46" holds only if the setup text is joined to the query and scored as one text. That is what `sym.py` does. `verdict.metrics` computes moving parts as `_count("SELECT") - 1 + ...` and steps as `FROM/JOIN targets - 1`, and it applies both offsets once per text. The verdict is the same (`SQL` either way), but the published figure is not. -> Pin "setup and query are joined and scored as one text" in AC-1/AC-5.
